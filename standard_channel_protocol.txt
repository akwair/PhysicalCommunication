EPA/EVA power-delay-profile validation protocol
Frozen before production runs, 2026-11-13.

Question: Does the residual-leakage / calibration-source relationship persist
under externally specified power-delay profiles, with receiver settings fixed?

Source: ETSI TS 136 101 V8.10.0 (2010-07), 3GPP TS 36.101 Release 8,
Annex B.2.1, Tables B.2.1-2 (EPA) and B.2.1-3 (EVA), printed page 138.
https://etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/136100_136199/136101/08.10.00_60/ts_136101v081000p.pdf
Use all seven EPA / nine EVA delays and relative powers without truncation.
Convert dB powers to linear and normalize total mean channel power to one.

Physical embedding: sampling interval 250 ns (4 MHz), N=64, 62.5 kHz spacing,
16-sample CP (4 us), 16 comb pilots, 48 QPSK data subcarriers. Physical delays
are not rescaled. EPA maximum 410 ns = 1.64 samples; EVA maximum 2510 ns =
10.04 samples. Both satisfy the existing receiver bound of 12 samples and
the four clean CP-pair condition. A 25-fold oversampled finite-burst audit
places every standard delay exactly on the 10 ns grid.
These are standard power-delay profiles embedded in the paper's block-static
OFDM model, not a full LTE conformance or standardized Doppler simulation.
Gains are independent proper complex Gaussian between T=16 calibration blocks
and between groups; delays and powers stay fixed. Use existing generate_cp,
including shared FFT/CP-tail noise. No external training dataset is involved.

Cells: EPA, EVA x SNR 0, 10, 20 dB. Three production seeds 1520261113,
1520261114, 1520261115; 300 independent groups per seed/cell, 900 pooled.
Smoke tests use other seeds and are excluded from production summaries.
No cell, seed or capped optimizer call is discarded based on its result.

Methods: integer EB and continuous-delay OMP/variable projection, each using
residual16, cp16 or known noise; additionally delay LS as a fixed baseline.
Keep L=12, max_delay=12, grid_step=.25, max_paths=6, and optimizer budget 60
as in the existing receiver. The six-path fitting cap is a receiver setting,
not the number of physical EPA/EVA taps. True delays, powers and path count
are never passed to these estimators. Known-noise is an information reference.
Compare on identical target observations within each group.

Outputs: all-cell normalized noise mean/std, analytic residual mean, NMSE
(ratio of total error to total truth energy), uncoded BER (96 bits/group),
paired CP-minus-residual improvements, and known-noise reference gaps.
2000 paired group-bootstrap replicates, resampled within each of the three
seeds, seed 1620261113. Report pointwise 95% percentile intervals without
multiplicity correction. Positive gain = lower error under the candidate.
Retain per-group metrics, noise estimates, selected orders, optimization
diagnostics, independent-seed summaries and hashes of the executed sources.
Audit physical CP cancellation and frequency-response agreement separately.
Write a compact all-six-cell table; interpretation must follow observed data.
